KlikBeam

Mirror layar iPhone atau HP Android ke headunit Android mobil lewat WiFi.

Bahasa IndonesiaEnglishKebijakan Privasi · Privacy Policy

Cara menyambung

  1. Pasang KlikDash (gratis) di headunit Android, versi 1.6.5 atau lebih baru.
  2. Sambungkan headunit ke hotspot iPhone.
  3. Di KlikDash buka ⚙ › Launcher › KLIKBEAM › BUKA PENERIMA. Layar menampilkan kode pairing 6 digit.
  4. Di aplikasi KlikBeam pilih headunit (atau ketik IP-nya), isi kode, lalu ketuk Tes koneksi.
  5. Ketuk Mulai Mirror. Di iPhone pilih KlikBeam › Mulai Siaran; di Android pilih Bagikan seluruh layar.

Tanya jawab

Headunit tidak muncul di daftar

Pastikan izin Jaringan Lokal untuk KlikBeam aktif (Settings › KlikBeam), dan headunit serta iPhone berada di jaringan yang sama. Bila tetap tidak muncul, ketik alamat IP yang tampil di layar KlikDash.

Bisakah HP dikontrol dari headunit?

Belum. KlikBeam hanya menampilkan layar (iOS memang tidak mengizinkan iPhone dikontrol dari perangkat lain).

Suaranya bagaimana?

Suara tetap lewat Bluetooth iPhone ke headunit, seperti biasa.

Netflix atau Apple TV+ tampil hitam

Konten yang diproteksi memang tidak bisa direkam layar oleh iOS.

iPhone terasa panas

Mirror membuat iPhone bekerja keras (merekam layar, encode video, WiFi). Sebaiknya sambil dicas, letakkan di tempat yang terkena AC, dan hentikan siaran bila iPhone terasa panas.

Keselamatan

Tonton video hanya saat mobil berhenti. Jangan mengoperasikan iPhone atau menonton video sambil menyetir.

Kontak

Laporkan masalah atau saran di GitHub (KlikDash issues).

How to connect

  1. Install KlikDash (free) on the Android headunit, version 1.6.5 or later.
  2. Connect the headunit to your iPhone's hotspot.
  3. In KlikDash open ⚙ › Launcher › KLIKBEAM › OPEN RECEIVER. The screen shows a 6-digit pairing code.
  4. In KlikBeam pick the headunit (or type its IP), enter the code, then tap Test connection.
  5. Tap Start Mirror. On iPhone choose KlikBeam › Start Broadcast; on Android choose Share entire screen.

FAQ

The headunit doesn't show up

Make sure Local Network access is on for KlikBeam (Settings › KlikBeam) and both devices are on the same network. If it still doesn't appear, type the IP address shown on the KlikDash screen.

Can I control the phone from the headunit?

Not yet. KlikBeam only shows the screen (iOS doesn't allow an iPhone to be controlled from another device).

What about sound?

Sound still goes over Bluetooth from the iPhone to the headunit, as usual.

Netflix or Apple TV+ shows black

iOS doesn't allow protected content to be screen recorded.

The iPhone gets hot

Mirroring works the iPhone hard (screen recording, video encoding, WiFi). Keep it charging, place it where the AC reaches it, and stop the broadcast if it feels hot.

Safety

Only watch video while the car is parked. Never operate the iPhone or watch video while driving.

KlikDash

A lightweight launcher for Android car headunits. An instrument-panel home screen at about 0% idle CPU, in place of the stock 3D animation.

  • Offline prayer times from GPS with a live countdown
  • Al-Qur'an with Mishary Alafasy recitation, downloaded per surah for offline use
  • TANYA voice assistant for car commands, with your own Claude or OpenAI-compatible key
  • Rearrangeable shortcuts, CPU temperature, power saver and in-app updates
Download KlikDash Source

Native, not bloated

Built with Tauri and Rust, so installers are a few megabytes and the apps start fast.

Signed and notarized

macOS builds are signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple. They open without a Gatekeeper warning.

Your keys, your data

Passwords and API keys live in your OS keychain. Bring your own AI provider; nothing goes through a middleman.
